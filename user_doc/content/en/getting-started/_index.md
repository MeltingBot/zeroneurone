# Getting Started

This section guides you through your first steps with ZeroNeurone.

## Guided tutorial

The easiest way to discover the application is the **built-in guided tutorial**: from the home page, click "Guided tutorial" in the welcome banner, or the graduation-cap icon in the footer. Anchored tooltips walk you through each step — creating a practice dossier, elements, a relationship, tags, properties, dated events, geolocation, and a tour of the Map, Timeline and Matrix views. Each step validates when you actually perform the action; you can skip any step or quit at any time, and the practice dossier can be deleted at the end.
